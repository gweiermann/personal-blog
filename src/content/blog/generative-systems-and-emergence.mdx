The most fascinating aspect of generative art is emergence—the way simple, local rules produce global complexity. No single line of code describes the final image; the outcome arises from the system as a whole.

Studying cellular automata, reaction-diffusion, and noise-based algorithms reveals a deep truth: beauty can be procedural. We design the rules; the system discovers the form.
