Less is more—a cliché that holds true in interface design. In digital spaces, minimalism isn't about stripping away until nothing remains; it's about removing everything that doesn't serve the user's goal.

Whitespace isn't empty. It's a design element that gives content room to breathe and directs attention. When we embrace minimalism, we create more focused and meaningful experiences.
